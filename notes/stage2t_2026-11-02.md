# Stage 2T: rank predictions tested on the Stage 1 runs, 2026-11-02

Predictions P1 and P2 were committed in the Stage 2 plan (`b4cff14`) before this ran. Script: `scripts/stage2t_mechanism.py`; raw output `results/stage2t.json`. Laptop RTX 3050, torch 2.15.0, memory cap 0.75; all 15 checkpoints rebuilt with `load_pretrained` (wiring fingerprints matched). Activity: brain state after each of the 6 layers on the first 16,384 validation tokens (64 fixed windows of 256); PR = tr(C)² / tr(C²) of the neuron covariance over tokens.

## Verdicts

- **P1 (Clark 2024) refuted here.** Last-layer full-brain PR, real ÷ shuf, by seed: 0.996, 0.931, 1.064 (mean about 1.0), against an effective-rank ratio of 0.48 (topology) or 0.85 (trained). Half the rank did not halve activity dimension in this driven, 6-step brain.
- **P2 (Liu 2024) not supported as stated.** Real changed more than shuf in 3/3 seeds (0.806, 0.814, 0.813 vs 0.800, 0.805, 0.804), but less than er (0.861) and dense (1.602). Dense starts from a different fan-in scale (760 neurons), so its number is not comparable.

## Observed, not pre-registered: the real wiring's readout is one step late

Real readout PR at layers 2–4 is 13.7, 29.1, 46.0 against 42.8, 56.9, 64.1 for shuf. Graph check (input = 256 lowest in-degree, readout = 256 highest; seed 0): the share of readout neurons one hop from an input is 0.195 for real, 0.961 and 0.949 for shuf seeds 0 and 1, 0.992 for er, 0.914 for real_randio; every arm reaches 1.000 in two hops. Direct input-to-readout edges: real 236, shuf 1,777 and 1,798, er 1,203, real_randio 902. The real mushroom body routes ALPN → KC → MBON, so its readout carries little input information until the third brain step. Hypothesis (open, untested): the real arm loses about one of six brain steps of useful readout, which would explain part of X_WORSE. A test would need retraining (for example real wiring with one extra brain step, or readout from layer 3 on); not planned.
