## Derived (PREREG §5)

- **SESOI = 0.01 nats.** 2 x SD(B0-M) = 0.0054, so the 0.01 floor binds. Per §5, effects below 0.02 nats get 5 seeds in later stages.
- **Ladder fit:** L = 8.031 x N^-0.05235 (N = non-embedding params). Max residual 0.0075 nats. With 3 points and 2 parameters, the fit has 1 degree of freedom.
- **Size multiplier at M** (how much larger B0-M must be to gain the stated amount): 0.01 nats -> 1.058x, 0.02 -> 1.119x, 0.05 -> 1.327x, 0.10 -> 1.767x. Training FLOPs at fixed tokens scale with N, so these are also compute multipliers.

## Observations (not pre-registered)

- The curve gets steeper, not flatter: S->M (N x2.02) gains 0.116 nats, M->L (N x2.13) gains 0.143. A pure power law cannot bend that way, which is where the 0.0075 residual comes from. Read the multipliers as approximate.
- B0-L seed SD (0.0099) is about 4x B0-M. A Stage 2 comparison at size L needs its own noise estimate or more seeds.
- Wall clock grows slower than N: L has 4.3x the parameters of S and takes 2.5x the time. At these sizes the fixed costs (embedding, head, data, optimizer step) are a large share.
